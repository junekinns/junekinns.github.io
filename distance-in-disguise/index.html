The Open Image-Editing Consistency Judge Is Mostly a Distance in Disguise Residual Accuracy Reveals What It Misses

June Kim1,2
1University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign 2SNOW Corp.
AAAI-27 Student Abstract
Paper (soon) Supplementary (soon) Poster (soon)

The open consistency judge is accurate where a training-free source distance is right, and drops toward chance where it is wrong. A current API model solves many of those residual pairs.

Abstract

Open VLM judges are used as RL rewards and benchmark evaluators for whether an image edit preserved what it should not change (consistency). We ask what such a judge has learned beyond a training-free rule, pick the output closer to the source. On EditReward-Bench the best of nine distance variants (DINOv2, 0.787) is not significantly different from a dedicated 8B consistency judge (0.794) and above the other open judges we tested (0.699–0.720). The judge is accurate where distance works (0.89–0.90 on pairs DINOv2 gets right) but not where it fails (0.44–0.61), on two benchmarks. We call the latter residual accuracy. Many residual pairs carry a learnable signal rather than pure label noise: a current API model (Gemini-3.8-Flash) reaches 0.72–0.77 on them while staying close to the open judge elsewhere, which accounts for 80–96% of its advantage. Aggregate accuracy hides this: a training trick that adds +6pp aggregate does not raise residual accuracy. We propose residual accuracy, reported jointly with accuracy on distance-right pairs, as a target for open consistency rewards.

Key Findings

  • 1A plain image distance to the source (DINOv2) is statistically tied with the best open consistency judge on EditReward-Bench (0.787 vs. 0.794), and beats the other open judges.
  • 2The open judge agrees with the distance on 75–82% of pairs. Where the distance is wrong, the judge is mostly wrong too (residual accuracy 0.44–0.61).
  • 3Residual pairs are not just label noise: Gemini-3.8-Flash gets 0.72–0.77 on them, which is 80–96% of its advantage over the open judge.
  • 4Aggregate accuracy can rise by making a judge more distance-like. Report residual accuracy next to it.
