AI Eraser

Designed the eraser system and built both of its APIs, masking and inpainting. Only the masked area is regenerated and blended seamlessly into the original.

With a standard loss, the inpainting LoRA kept redrawing erased objects and drifting color at the mask boundary, and more data didn't help. Reweighting the mask interior and boundary, plus a term that keeps everything outside the mask unchanged, reduced both noticeably.

Tested masking backbones of several sizes. A smaller one with bf16 and GPU-side transforms cut the mask step about 8×, but side-by-side quality checks sent it back to the larger one.

Generative Retouching

Probed a reference lip-gloss effect with crafted inputs. It added gloss and fine texture that were not in the input, so no texture overlay or shader could produce it. Reproduced it with a LoRA on a distilled FLUX-family model trained from a handful of before/after pairs.

A LoRA trained on the base model lost much of its effect under the four-step distilled sampler; training at those four noise levels with perceptual and adversarial losses recovered it. Built a web tool where artists upload before/after pairs and get a trained, testable effect without an engineer.

AI Cutout

Added domain routing to cutout's segmentation. A lightweight router classifies each image and picks the base model or a domain-tuned adapter, so one deployment also handles graphics and patterns the base model struggled with. In the weeks after launch, the feature's save and subscription rates doubled.

Training and evaluation data comes from recurring failures in real user images, checked by people, because retraining on the model's own labels had stopped helping.
