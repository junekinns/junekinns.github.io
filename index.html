Paid AI editing features for SNOW's camera apps, serving millions of requests a month.

AI Eraser

Designed the eraser system and built both of its APIs, masking and inpainting. Only the masked area is regenerated and blended seamlessly into the original.

With a standard loss, the inpainting LoRA kept redrawing erased objects and drifting color at the mask boundary, and more data didn't help. Reweighting the mask interior and boundary, plus a term that keeps everything outside the mask unchanged, reduced both noticeably.

Tested masking backbones of several sizes and chose the one that best balanced quality and serving cost.

AI Cutout

Segmentation models for cutout. A lightweight router classifies each image and picks the base model or a domain-tuned adapter, so one deployment covers image domains the base was never trained for.

Training and evaluation data comes from recurring failures in real user images, with human review in the loop, since the model stopped improving on its own labels.
