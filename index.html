Generative Makeup

Trained a LoRA on a distilled FLUX-family model from a few before/after pairs to reproduce a lip-gloss effect.

A LoRA trained on the base model lost most of the effect under the distilled four-step sampler; training at the sampler's own noise levels fixed it. Built a web tool where designers upload before/after pairs and get a trained effect back.

AI Eraser

Designed the object removal pipeline and built both of its services, segmentation and inpainting. Only the masked region is regenerated and blended back into the original.

With a standard loss, the inpainting LoRA kept redrawing erased objects and shifting color at the mask boundary. Reweighting the loss over the mask and adding a term that keeps the outside unchanged reduced both.

Tested segmentation backbones of several sizes. A smaller one with bf16 and GPU-side preprocessing ran about 8× faster, but side-by-side quality checks sent it back to the larger one.
